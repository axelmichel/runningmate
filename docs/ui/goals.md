# Goals

A goal is a target you set yourself: a distance, a time, a climb or a number of activities. You can reach it in one
activity ("a 10 km run in under 50 minutes") or as the total over a period ("100 km a month", "3 runs a week",
"500 km in 60 days"), once or again and again.

## Setting a goal
Open the **Goals** page from the sidebar or with ⇧⌘O and click **New goal**.

- **Sport**: all activities, or only runs, rides or walks.
- **Goal for**: distance (km), time (hours, the elapsed time of the activities), elevation (m climbed) or the number of
  activities.
- **Target**: the amount to reach.
- **Counted**: *all activities together* adds them up over the period; *in one activity* needs a single activity to
  reach the target. A number of activities is always a total.
- **Time limit**: for a distance or climb in one activity, the most it may take, as H:MM:SS. "10 km" with 0:50:00 is
  reached by any activity of at least 10 km that took 50 minutes or less.
- **Period**: a week (Monday to Sunday), a calendar month or year, a number of days, or no period (from the start date
  to the end date, or for good).
- **Repeat**: the goal starts over in the next period. Without it, the goal has a single period and is reached or
  missed once.
- **Start** and **End**: the first day and, optionally, the last day counted. Weeks, months and years follow the
  calendar, so the week, month or year holding the start date counts in full. A number of days runs from the start
  date. A start date in the past counts the activities you already did.
- **Name**: left empty, the goal is named after what it asks for, for example "100 km every month".

## Progress
The dashboard shows a row of chips, one per goal that is running or reached, with the current period's progress, what
is left to do and the days left. Click a chip to open the Goals page. When you move the dashboard back in time (the
arrows and date field below the photos, four weeks a step), the chips show the goals as they stood on that day.

On the Goals page each goal has a card with the current period, the earlier periods as small bars (filled where the
target was reached), how many periods were reached, the streak of periods reached in a row, the number of activities
with a star and the latest of them. Filter by sport at the top. You can edit, archive or delete a goal from its card.
Archived goals are hidden from the dashboard and the list unless you tick **Show archived**, but keep their stars.
Deleting a goal removes its stars.

## Stars
An activity that hit a goal shows a star, on the dashboard feed and in the activity panel. Hover it to see which goals
it hit.

- For a goal in one activity, every activity that reaches it gets a star.
- For a total, the activity that took the total past the target in its period gets the star.

Progress and stars are worked out from the activities each time, so importing, editing or deleting an activity updates
them right away.
