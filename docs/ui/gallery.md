# Gallery

The gallery shows the photos and videos of all activities in one place, newest activity first.

## The Gallery page
Open it from the sidebar or with ⇧⌘P. Filter by sport at the top; the number of photos and videos shown is at the
top right. **Grid** shows everything as one grid of tiles; **By activity** groups them under the activity's title and
date. The choice is kept. In **By activity**, clicking the title opens the activity in the
[detail window](detail-window.md), where previous and next move to the neighbouring activities with photos. Videos show
their first frame with a play symbol. Files that no longer exist (for example a linked original that was moved) are left
out.

## Full view
Click a tile to see it in full. Move through the photos and videos with the arrows beside it, the arrow keys or the strip
of thumbnails at the bottom. **Slideshow** moves on by itself, every few seconds for a photo and at the end of a video;
the space bar starts and stops it while a photo is shown. **Open activity** opens the activity the photo belongs to.

Photos and videos are added to an activity on its edit page; the photos in the detail window open in the same full view.
